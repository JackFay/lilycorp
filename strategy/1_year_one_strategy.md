The acquisition of Lily Corporation is an **analog-to-digital transition**, not a startup turnaround or a venture-backed software play. For 44 years (since 1982), Lily Corp has operated as a high-margin, word-of-mouth lifestyle manufacturer of heavy-duty positive-displacement chemical injection equipment and custom molded accessories.

## Core Pillar 1: Dual-Channel Frictionless Ordering

**Problem:** Lily Corp loses sales whenever the Aurora office is closed (before 8:00 AM CST, evenings, and weekends). Meanwhile, Maggie M spends 15–20 hours per week manually keying handwritten or emailed PDF purchase orders into QuickBooks Desktop, creating bottlenecks and transcription errors.

**Solution:** Deploy a **Dual-Channel Smart Ordering System** that meets contractors exactly where they already work, requiring **zero customer workflow disruption** and **no forced account logins**.

                   │                                                          │

                         ▼                                               ▼
          Customer receives clean order confirmation            Warehouse packing ticket auto-prints

### Channel A: Jobsite QR Codes & Mobile Quick-Reorder (`orders.lilycorp.com`)
1. **Target Catalog:** A streamlined, mobile-optimized catalog restricted strictly to the **Top 50 high-margin consumables and wear parts** (static mixers, surface ports, corner ports, tubing adapters, fluid hoses, check valves, and flush kits). Custom proportioning machines and engineered rigs continue to route to Dave J and Bob for phone consultation.
2. **Field Distribution via Equipment Decals:** Print heavy-duty, chemical- and oil-resistant vinyl stickers applied to:
   * Every machine leaving the Aurora shop (new builds and repairs).
   * Every rental machine shipped into the field.
   * Master carton boxes of consumable parts.
   * *Decal Call-to-Action:* `"Need replacement nozzles, ports, or wear parts? Scan here to reorder from your phone in 30 seconds."`
3. **Frictionless Checkout:** Pre-populated customer profile matching phone number/email to existing Net-30 credit terms or stored credit cards via Stripe Billing.

### Channel B: The AI Email Order Desk (`orders@lilycorp.com`)
1. **The Contractor Reality:** Over 65% of legacy civil contractors will never create an online account. They send casual emails from jobsite trucks (*"Dave, send 4 boxes of 1/2-inch mixers and 200 ports to the Joliet bridge site, PO 8841"*) or have their back office attach a PDF Purchase Order.
2. **Autonomous Ingestion Worker:**
   * **Fuzzy Customer Lookup:** Matches incoming email domain, sender contact, or company name to the QuickBooks customer master.
   * **Intelligent Line-Item Extraction:** LLM parser extracts part descriptions, quantities, and customer PO numbers from email bodies and attached PDF purchase orders.
   * **Tiered Pricing & Terms Verification:** Automatically applies the customer’s historical discount schedule (e.g., Tier 1 Contractor vs. Distributor) and verifies Net-30 standing.
   * **Draft Staging in QuickBooks:** Creates a **DRAFT Sales Order / Invoice** directly inside QuickBooks.
3. **The Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard (Staff Buy-In):**
   * The AI never sends invoices or communicates autonomously with customers without human approval.
   * At 8:00 AM, Maggie opens her screen to find pre-staged drafts:
     > *"Midwest Bridge Corp sent PO #9012 for 300 Surface Ports and 2 Boxes of Mixers ($1,850). QuickBooks Draft #10941 staged."*  
     > `[Approve & Send]` / `[Edit]`
   * Maggie’s order processing time drops from 15 minutes to **under 30 seconds per order**, eliminating 15–20 hours/week of data-entry drudgery.

In recent years, rental revenue collapsed below $40,000 simply because Bob Trout stopped quoting rentals. Site visit verification confirmed that multiple pre-built, fully assembled rental machines sit on Aurora shelves right now, 100% paid for and gathering dust.

### Execution Protocol
1. **Inventory Audit & Fleet Tagging (Month 1):** Julian and Dave K inspect, pressure-test, and catalog all shelf units (model numbers, pressure ratios, motor configurations).
2. **Internal Rental Availability Board:** Create a shared, real-time fleet dashboard for Dave J and Maggie showing which machines are in stock, currently deployed, or scheduled for return.
3. **The Rent-to-Own (RTO) Incentive:**
   * Civil contractors bidding on short 2-to-8-week DOT repair contracts frequently resist spending $15,000–$22,000 on capital equipment.
   * Standard Offer: **50% of all rental payments apply directly toward the purchase price** if purchased within 60 days.
   * *The Commercial Result:* Transforms temporary renters into long-term equipment owners who are permanently locked into Lily Corp's proprietary wear parts, packings, and accessories.
4. **Mandatory Consumable Bundling:** Every rental shipment must leave Aurora bundled with 250 surface ports, 2 boxes of static mixers, and pump flush solvent, driving immediate high-margin consumable pull-through.
5. **Financial Sizing:** Deploying an average of 4 to 6 units per month adds **+$100,000 to $160,000/year** in 85% gross margin cash flow.

## Core Pillar 5: Turn-Key Resin Pallet Bundling (Buy-and-Resell)

**Problem:** Contractors cannot inject cracks without resin (epoxy or polyurethane). When contractors buy a dispenser or ports from Lily Corp, they must separately source 5-gallon pails or drums of structural resin from chemical distributors, causing logistics friction and multiple freight bills.

**Solution:** Avoid the multi-year bureaucracy of corporate OEM co-branding agreements. Instead, execute a pragmatic **Buy-and-Resell Stocking Model** using local distributors and specialized manufacturers.

```
[Contractor Order] ──► [Lily Corp Aurora Facility] ──► [Single Bill of Lading (Pallet)]
Dispenser/Rental       • Assembles machine             • 1x Lily Corp Proportioner
Ports & Mixers         • Pulls ports & mixers          • 500x Surface Ports
Chemical Resin         • Adds pre-stocked resin pails  • 4x Pails Sikadur 35 or Prime Flex
```

### Sourcing & Vendor Roadmap
1. **Stage 1 (Months 3–6): Regional Buy-and-Resell (Zero Setup Friction)**
   * Open commercial dealer accounts with **Chicago Contractor's Supply (CCS)** in Naperville (20 mins away) and **White Cap**.
   * Stock 2 to 4 pallets of the two most specified crack repair resins:
     * **Sika Sikadur 35 Hi-Mod LV** (industry standard for structural epoxy crack injection).
     * **Prime Resins Prime Flex 920** (standard for water cut-off and polyurethane soil stabilization).
   * Bundle resin at contractor list price (capturing a 20%–25% distribution margin) on Lily pallet shipments.
2. **Stage 2 (Months 6–12): Direct Specialty Manufacturer Account**
   * Partner directly with **Prime Resins** (Conyers, GA / RPM International family). Prime Resins specializes exclusively in chemical injection grouting and actively seeks equipment OEM partners.
   * Explore direct relationship with **Simpson Strong-Tie** via their West Chicago manufacturing and training facility (20 mins from Aurora).
3. **Value Proposition to Contractor:**
   * *"Everything you need to shoot concrete cracks on Monday morning, pre-palletized, pre-tested, and delivered on a single invoice."*
4. **Financial Sizing:** Bundling resin on just 20% to 25% of equipment shipments adds **+$120,000 to $220,000** in gross revenue (25%–30% gross margin), increasing average order value by $2,500 to $4,500.
