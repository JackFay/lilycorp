**Re: Non-Binding Letter of Intent – Acquisition of Substantially All Assets of Lily Corporation**

Dear Bob, John, Lily, and Michael:

This Letter of Intent (this "**Letter**" or "**LOI**") sets forth the preliminary terms and conditions under which John "Jack" Fay, or an affiliated acquisition entity to be formed (the "**Buyer**"), proposes to acquire substantially all of the operating assets and business of Lily Corporation, an Illinois corporation (the "**Company**" or the "**Seller**"), whose legal equity is held by Lily Trout, Michael Trout, and related family holders (collectively, the "**Shareholders**"), managed and operated by Robert "Bob" Trout, President (together with John T. Trout, the "**Key Principals**").

We appreciate the time you have spent introducing us to Lily Corporation’s products, facility, and distinguished 40-year history. Our objective is to present a clean, compelling, and streamlined transaction structure that provides the Shareholders and Key Principals with maximum certainty of close and a definitive retirement exit, while eliminating transaction friction, complicated audit adjustments, and post-closing administrative burdens.

---

### 1. Transaction Structure
Buyer will acquire substantially all of the assets, properties, and business operations of the Company through an **Asset Purchase Agreement** (the "**Definitive Agreement**") free and clear of all liens, claims, encumbrances, and liabilities. The parties agree to negotiate in good faith an allocation of the Purchase Price among the Purchased Assets in accordance with Section 1060 of the Internal Revenue Code prior to execution of the Definitive Agreement.

* **Purchased Assets:** The assets to be transferred to Buyer at the closing of the transaction (the "**Closing**") shall include, without limitation:
  1. All manufacturing machinery, tooling, injection molding presses, material dryers, servo motors, welding equipment, delivery vehicles, forklifts, pre-built rental machines, shop equipment, replacement parts, and accessories.
  2. Complete, unencumbered, one-hundred percent (100%) legal title and ownership of all **ten (10) proprietary injection molds** utilized in the production of the Company’s proprietary catalog of custom molded components.
  3. All raw materials, work-in-process, supplies, and finished goods inventory on-site or in-transit as of Closing (subject to Section 2 and Section 3).
  4. All customer accounts, historical customer purchase records, vendor contracts, technical drawings, machine setup specifications, bills of materials (BOMs), and custom proportioner quoting formulas.
  5. All intellectual property, trademarks, trade secrets, domain names, websites, telephone numbers, marketing collateral, exclusive rights to the registered corporate name "**Lily Corporation**", and general corporate goodwill.
* **Excluded Assets:** Cash and cash equivalents, personal effects, corporate minute books, tax seals, personal life insurance policies of the Shareholders or Key Principals, and Pre-Closing Receivables (as defined below).
* **Accounts Receivable (AR) Treatment:** All accounts receivable generated from goods shipped, products sold, or services rendered prior to the Closing Date ("**Pre-Closing Receivables**") shall be retained by Seller. Following Closing, Buyer agrees to assist Seller as a collection agent for a period of ninety (90) days by promptly endorsing and remitting to Seller any customer payments received that specifically apply to Pre-Closing Receivables; provided, however, that Buyer assumes no credit risk, guarantee of collection, or obligation to institute collection proceedings on Seller's behalf. Buyer reserves the option, exercisable during Due Diligence, to purchase bona fide current Pre-Closing Receivables (aged under 60 days) at Closing at face value with full ninety (90) day right of recourse against the Indemnification Holdback.
* **Excluded Liabilities (Zero Accounts Payable Assumed):** Buyer will assume **no liabilities, debts, or obligations** of the Company or Seller, whether known or unknown, accrued, absolute, or contingent. Specifically:
  1. **Accounts Payable:** Buyer assumes **zero** trade payables or operating liabilities. Seller and Shareholders shall satisfy and pay in full at or prior to Closing all outstanding accounts payable, supplier balances, vendor invoices, utility bills, and contractor obligations incurred prior to Closing.
  2. **Indebtedness, Taxes & Liens:** Seller and Shareholders shall satisfy and pay in full at or prior to Closing all federal, state, and local tax liabilities, accrued employee compensation or vacation/benefit obligations, bank debts, and any outstanding Small Business Administration (SBA) disaster or EIDL loans, providing clean UCC-1 lien terminations and payoff letters at Closing.
  3. **Customer Deposits & Prepayments:** Buyer shall receive a dollar-for-dollar credit at Closing against the cash due for any pre-closing customer deposits, retainers, or advance payments held by Seller for unfilled customer orders or equipment in progress.

---

### 2. Purchase Price & Pre-Closing Inventory Verification
The purchase price for the Purchased Assets shall be a baseline sum of **$1,050,000 (One Million Fifty Thousand Dollars)** (the "**Purchase Price**").

* **Purchased Inventory Scope:** The Purchase Price includes one hundred percent (100%) of the Company's usable, merchantable operating inventory, raw materials, components, and finished goods on-site or in-transit as of the Closing Date, consistent with normal operating levels.
* **Pre-Closing Physical Count & Review:** To satisfy standard lender underwriting requirements and ensure an accurate asset transfer, Buyer and Seller shall conduct a customary joint physical inventory count and review during the pre-closing due diligence period. The Definitive Agreement shall incorporate customary mutual provisions establishing an agreed operating inventory baseline and standard mechanisms to ensure that transferred inventory consists of active, usable, and merchantable stock consistent with past practices.

---

### 3. Ordinary Course Operation & Working Inventory Covenant
To preserve business continuity and ensure adequate operating stock at Closing, Seller, Shareholders, and Key Principals covenant and agree that:
1. Between the date of this Letter and Closing, the Company shall operate strictly in the **ordinary course of business** consistent with historical practices.
2. Seller shall maintain standard purchasing, restocking, and replenishment cycles for resins, components, parts, and consumables to ensure normal operating inventory levels at Closing.
3. Seller shall not conduct any extraordinary bulk discount sales, scrap liquidations, or accelerated order fulfillment outside the ordinary course of business that would materially deplete physical inventory prior to Closing.

---

### 4. Sources of Funds & Payment Terms
The Purchase Price of $1,050,000 shall be satisfied at Closing under one of two mutually agreed structures, subject to lender approval:

* **Structure A (Senior SBA Loan + Third-Party Escrow):**
  1. **Senior SBA Loan (90%):** $945,000 funded via a ten-year senior acquisition term loan under the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) 7(a) loan program (which may also include an additional working capital facility).
  2. **Buyer Cash Equity (10%):** $105,000 cash injected by Buyer at Closing.
  3. **Indemnification Escrow Holdback:** At Closing, $105,000 (10%) of loan proceeds will be deposited into a third-party escrow account administered by an independent escrow agent for a period of twelve (12) months to secure Seller’s customary representations, warranties, and indemnities.
* **Structure B (Senior SBA Loan + Standby Seller Note):**
  1. **Senior SBA Loan (80%):** $840,000 funded via an SBA 7(a) term loan.
  2. **Buyer Cash Equity (10%):** $105,000 cash injected by Buyer at Closing.
  3. **Subordinated Seller Note (10%):** $105,000 promissory note issued by Buyer to Seller on full standby terms (no principal or interest payments for 24 months per SBA guidelines), bearing 5.0% simple interest per annum, subject to standard SBA Form 155 subordination and Buyer's right of set-off for indemnifiable claims.

---

### 5. Transition Services & Post-Closing Customer Handover
To ensure complete operational readiness and preserve business confidentiality:

1. **Pre-Closing Operational Learning Period:**
   * Commencing upon execution of the Definitive Agreement and continuing through Closing (an estimated 30-to-60 day period), Robert Trout agrees to provide on-site instruction and operational training at the Aurora facility (up to 3 to 4 days per week).
   * **Scope of Pre-Closing Training:** The pre-closing period shall be strictly dedicated to internal operations and business knowledge transfer, including:
     * Training Buyer in day-to-day manufacturing operations, machine maintenance, shop workflows, and quality assurance.
     * Documenting proprietary proportioner quoting algorithms, resin/epoxy ratio specifications, equipment bills of materials (BOMs), and machinery setup parameters.
     * Shadowing key operating personnel (Dave Jepsen, Dave Kling, Julian P, Maggie M, and Greg E) to ensure operational continuity and complete familiarity with daily shop routines.
   * **Confidentiality & Internal Focus:** To protect the Company's business relationships and maintain strict confidentiality, **no customer outreach, contact, or introductions shall occur prior to Closing**.
2. **Post-Closing Commercial Handover & Advisory Availability:**
   * **Relationship Introductions & Transition Support:** Following Closing, Robert Trout shall make himself reasonably available on an as-needed advisory basis to assist Buyer with warm introductions to key customer accounts, primary suppliers, and industry contacts as deemed appropriate or requested by Buyer (via introductory email, correspondence, or telephone). The parties' mutual intent is to ensure commercial continuity while keeping transitional outreach streamlined, practical, and focused strictly on accounts where an introduction is necessary or beneficial. The specific scope and reasonable duration of any post-closing advisory availability shall be set forth in the Definitive Agreement.
   * **No Post-Close Payroll:** The parties' mutual objective is that all material knowledge transfer and operational training shall be substantially completed prior to Closing. The Company will have no ongoing salary, W-2 payroll, benefits, or consulting retainer obligations post-close ($0.00 post-close payroll).

---

### 6. Commercial Facility Lease Terms
In connection with Closing, Buyer (or its operating entity) and the property owner of the Aurora, Illinois facility shall execute a commercial triple-net lease agreement containing the following terms:

1. **Base Rent:** $68,000 per annum ($5,666.67 per month), payable monthly.
2. **Lease Structure:** Triple Net (NNN), with Tenant responsible for pro-rata real estate taxes, building hazard insurance, and routine interior maintenance (consistent with historical Company expense treatment).
3. **Term and Renewal Options (SBA SOP 50 10 Compliance):** To satisfy SBA lending requirements that the real estate lease match the ten-year loan amortization, the lease shall provide an **initial term of five (5) years, plus one (1) consecutive five (5) year renewal option** exercisable at Tenant’s sole discretion. Annual rent escalations shall be capped at the lesser of 2.5% per annum or the Consumer Price Index (CPI).
4. **Right of First Refusal (ROFR):** Tenant shall receive an exclusive Right of First Refusal to purchase the commercial real estate on terms identical to any bona fide third-party offer received by Landlord during the lease term or renewal term.
5. **Facility Condition and Environmental Warranty:** Landlord shall warrant that the roof, structural foundation, HVAC, electrical, and plumbing systems are in sound working order as of Closing. Landlord/Seller shall indemnify Buyer for any pre-existing environmental liabilities, subject to a satisfactory Phase I Environmental Site Assessment (ESA).
6. **SBA Landlord Waiver:** Landlord agrees to execute the bank's standard SBA Landlord Waiver and Collateral Access Agreement, permitting the lender temporary access to inspect or remove equipment collateral in the event of loan default.

### 8. Restrictive Covenants
At Closing, Robert Trout, John Trout, and all Shareholders shall execute a customary **five (5) year Non-Competition, Non-Solicitation, and Confidentiality Agreement**. 

* **Scope:** Restricts the Shareholders and Key Principals, directly or indirectly, from engaging in, owning, managing, consulting for, financing, or conducting educational seminars for any business that manufactures, sells, designs, or services chemical proportioners, pressure injection equipment, specialty dispensing accessories, or related tooling across the continental United States.
* **Non-Solicitation:** Prohibits soliciting or hiring any employees, active customers, or key suppliers of the Company for five (5) years post-closing.

---

### 9. Representations, Warranties & Conditions to Closing
The Definitive Agreement shall contain customary representations and warranties for a transaction of this nature, including without limitation:
1. **Tooling & Mold Ownership:** Legal verification that all ten (10) injection molds are owned 100% free and clear by Lily Corporation with zero customer, vendor, or tooling shop liens.
2. **Financial Statement Integrity:** Reconciliation of Company QuickBooks general ledgers with federal tax returns (IRS Form 1120-S) from 2020 to present, verified via IRS Form 4506-C tax transcripts.
3. **Clear Tax and Lien Status:** Delivery of Certificates of Good Standing from the Illinois Secretary of State, tax clearances from the Illinois Department of Revenue (including Form CBS-1 Bulk Sales release) and IDES, and clean UCC-1 lien termination statements.
4. **Environmental Clearance:** Receipt of an SBA Phase I Environmental Site Assessment indicating no recognized environmental conditions (RECs).
5. **Financing & Bank Appraisals:** Buyer obtaining SBA 7(a) financing on terms acceptable to Buyer, supported by a satisfactory independent business valuation and equipment appraisal commissioned by the lender.
6. **No Material Adverse Change:** No substantial casualty loss, destruction of facilities or equipment, or material adverse change to the business operations or key customer relationships prior to Closing.
7. **Bulk Sales Act Compliance:** Seller shall comply with the Illinois Bulk Sales Act (or deliver a valid waiver) and provide all required creditor notifications prior to Closing.
8. **Personal Guaranty of Fundamental Representations:** The Shareholders (Lily Trout and Michael Trout) and Key Principals (Robert Trout and John T. Trout) shall personally, jointly and severally, guaranty Seller's indemnification obligations solely with respect to breaches of fundamental representations, including (a) clear title to Purchased Assets and injection molds, (b) tax liabilities and undisclosed indebtedness, (c) environmental liabilities, and (d) intentional fraud or willful misrepresentation. Such personal guaranty shall survive any post-closing dissolution of the Company for the applicable survival period set forth in Section 10. All other general representations and warranties shall be secured exclusively by the Indemnification Holdback (Section 4).

---

### 10. Indemnification & Survival
Seller, Shareholders, and Key Principals shall jointly and severally indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Buyer from and against all losses, liabilities, claims, and expenses arising from (a) any breach of Seller's representations, warranties, or covenants, (b) any Excluded Liabilities, and (c) any pre-closing tax obligations or environmental liabilities. General representations and warranties shall survive Closing for eighteen (18) months; representations relating to taxes, environmental matters, and title to assets shall survive for thirty-six (36) months. Claims shall first be satisfied from the Indemnification Holdback (Section 4) before recourse to personal guarantees. Specific indemnification caps, baskets, and claims procedures shall be set forth in the Definitive Agreement.

---

### 11. Dispute Resolution
Any dispute arising out of or relating to the Definitive Agreement shall first be submitted to good-faith mediation in Kane County, Illinois. If not resolved within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be submitted to binding arbitration administered by the American Arbitration Association under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, with the prevailing party entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

---

### 12. Due Diligence Period & Access
Buyer and his designated legal, accounting, and financing advisors shall have a period of **forty-five (45) days** following the execution of this Letter (the "**Due Diligence Period**") to complete confirmatory commercial, financial, tax, legal, and operational due diligence. Seller will provide full access to company books, records, historical financial files, raw QuickBooks backups, customer lists, vendor agreements, and facilities during normal business hours upon reasonable notice.

---

### 13. Definitive Agreements
Promptly following execution of this Letter, Buyer's counsel will deliver a draft Asset Purchase Agreement reflecting the terms herein. The parties agree to use good faith, commercially reasonable efforts to finalize definitive documentation and close the transaction within **ninety (90) days** of the date hereof.

---

### 14. Exclusivity (No-Shop)
In consideration of the substantial resources, professional expenses, and time Buyer will commit to due diligence, legal drafting, and financing underwriting, Seller, Shareholders, and Key Principals agree that for a period of **ninety (90) days** from the date of mutual execution of this Letter, they will not, directly or indirectly:
1. Solicit, initiate, facilitate, or encourage any inquiries, proposals, or offers regarding the sale of the Company, its equity, or its assets; or
2. Participate in any discussions, provide information to, or enter into any agreement with any third party regarding an acquisition, merger, or recapitalization of the Company.

---

### 15. Transaction Expenses & Deposit
Each party shall pay their own respective attorney, accounting, and advisory fees. Buyer shall pay all standard loan application, lender business valuation, and Phase I Environmental fees. Escrow fees (if applicable) shall be shared equally (50/50). Upon mutual execution of this Letter, Buyer shall deposit **$20,000 (Twenty Thousand Dollars)** (the "**Earnest Deposit**") into a third-party escrow account. The Earnest Deposit shall be fully refundable to Buyer at any time during the Due Diligence Period (Section 12) for any reason or no reason. Following expiration of the Due Diligence Period, the Earnest Deposit shall become non-refundable except upon failure of a financing contingency (Section 9.5) or a material breach of Seller's obligations hereunder. At Closing, the Earnest Deposit shall be credited against Buyer's cash equity injection and applied to the Purchase Price.

---

### 16. Confidentiality
The terms, conditions, and existence of this Letter, as well as all proprietary business and financial information exchanged during due diligence, shall be kept strictly confidential by the parties and their professional advisors, except as required by applicable law or to obtain transaction financing.

---

### 17. Non-Binding Nature; Binding Provisions
Except for **Sections 14 (Exclusivity), 15 (Transaction Expenses & Deposit), 16 (Confidentiality), 17 (Non-Binding Nature; Binding Provisions), and 18 (Governing Law)**, which shall constitute legally binding and enforceable obligations of the parties upon execution, this Letter is an expression of preliminary mutual intent only and does not create a binding legal obligation to consummate the transaction. Consummation of the acquisition remains subject to the execution and delivery of mutually satisfactory definitive agreements.

---

### 18. Governing Law
This Letter of Intent and all related matters shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Illinois, without giving effect to conflict of laws principles.
