# Lily Corp Acquisition: Ranked Deal Requirements

| Rank | Requirement | Risk Category | Strategic Rationale / Why It Sits Here |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **#1** | **QuickBooks Matches Tax Returns** | *Existential Solvency* | **Deal killer.** If the financials are fraudulent or if the 2024/2025 losses conceal a failing business, there is no deal. Furthermore, Chase will immediately reject your SBA loan without matching tax transcripts. |
| **#2** | **All 5 Employees Agree to Stay** | *Operational Survival* | **Deal killer.** Lily Corp has **zero written SOPs**. Dave K, Dave J, Maggie, Julian, and Greg *are* the operating manual. If Dave K or Dave J walks out on Day 1, the business halts. |
| **#3** | **Bob Stays 6–12 Months to Train You** | *Revenue Continuity* | Bob holds 40 years of customer trust, pricing formulas, and seminar credibility in his head. Without a warm handover, customer churn could wipe out 30%–50% of sales in Year 1. |
| **#4** | **Inventory Age Proof (> 2 Yrs = $0)** | *Overpayment Protection* | The $1.25M price includes inventory. Their 2025 write-down proved they hoarded dead stock dating back to 1982. You cannot borrow money at 10% SBA rates to buy obsolete plastic. |
| **#5** | **Clean Ownership of the 10 Molds** | *Asset Integrity* | The molds make the 25 custom parts. If customers own the tooling, they can pull them and walk away at any time, destroying the catalog. |
| **#6** | **Manufacturing Process Works & SOPs** | *Production Capability* | Prevents paying for "ghost" products that haven't been built in years or that rely on discontinued components. |
| **#7** | **Strict 5-Year Non-Compete** | *Competitive Moat* | Prevents Bob & John from taking your $1.25M and immediately launching a competing firm or consulting for a rival across town. |
| **#8** | **Aurora Facility Lease & Phase I ESA** | *Physical Security* | Moving heavy machinery and injection mold presses costs $100k+ and causes months of downtime. Plus, SBA mandates Phase I environmental clearance. |
| **#9** | **Clean Taxes & UCC Lien Searches** | *Legal Shield* | Protects you from inheriting hidden state sales tax liabilities or secret creditor liens against the machinery. |
| **#10** | **10%–15% Escrow Holdback (Year 1)** | *Post-Close Safety Net* | Crucial financial cushion to claw back cash for hidden bills, warranty spikes, or bad inventory that surfaces after closing. |
