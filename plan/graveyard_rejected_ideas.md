# The Graveyard: Rejected Products & Strategies
*(Documented Autopsies of Unviable Concepts)*

> **Purpose:** A world-class turnaround strategy is defined by what it refuses to do. When the product committee or adversarial evaluators reject an initiative, it is permanently logged here with the economic, operational, or customer rationale to prevent the company from ever wasting capital on it.

---

## 1. Shelved Pre-Acquisition Concepts

### Concept: "Smart IoT" Ratio-Logging Telemetry Rigs
* **Classification:** `REJECTED / UNVIABLE`
* **Original Idea:** Embed microcontrollers (Raspberry Pi/ESP32) and digital flow sensors on CD-250 rigs to stream real-time ratio data to a mobile app.
* **Autopsy & Reason for Rejection:**
  1. *Severe Operating Environment:* Chemical injection rigs pump highly abrasive, corrosive epoxies and polyurethanes at 1,500–2,500 PSI on messy bridge jobsites. Digital pressure transducers fail and gum up constantly.
  2. *Excessive R&D & Product Liability:* Introducing electronic sensor failure introduces liability if a bridge contractor claims a failed joint was caused by a false sensor reading.
  3. *Contractor Workflow:* Civil contractors use physical stroke counters and mechanical pressure gauges. They do not want Bluetooth pairing on a highway shoulder.
* **Alternative Approved Wedge:** Heavy-duty mechanical ratio verification kits and calibrated visual cylinder gauges.

---

### Concept: Consumer-Style E-Commerce Booking & Web Portal
* **Classification:** `REJECTED / UNVIABLE`
* **Original Idea:** Build an online shopping cart where contractors create accounts and buy $15,000 proportioners with credit cards.
* **Autopsy & Reason for Rejection:**
  1. *Procurement Mismatch:* Civil general contractors buy on Net 30/60 terms against formal Purchase Orders issued by corporate purchasing departments.
  2. *Friction Kills Conversion:* Forcing bridge foremen to remember web passwords causes them to call a competitor instead.
* **Alternative Approved Wedge:** Dual-Channel ordering: Jobsite QR codes on rigs that autofill SMS/email reorder drafts, and an automated PDF PO parser for Maggie's desk.

41: ### Concept: Lily "AeroLift" 240V Heated Polyurethane Slab Jacking Trailer Rig
42: * **Classification:** `REJECTED / UNVIABLE` (Logged in Cycle #4 - Market Expansion)
43: * **Original Premise:** Modify the CD-250 chassis with dual 6kW electric primary pre-heaters, an onboard transformer, and a 200-ft continuous heated hose package to build turnkey $65k–$100k mobile slab lifting trailer rigs against Graco Reactor (E-20/E-30) and PMC.
44: * **Autopsy & Operational Failure Modes:**
45:   1. *Shop Floor Paralysis:* Lily fabricator Dave Kling and assembler Julian Perez would spend 45+ labor hours per unit assembling complex electrical junction boxes, heating manifolds, and transformer wiring, halting core CD-250 and injection port production.
46:   2. *Regulatory & Certification Capex:* UL/CSA 508A electrical certification for high-amperage heating equipment requires ~$40k in testing fees and engineering documentation.
47:   3. *Warranty & 24/7 Field Support Liability:* When a heated hose blows an internal thermocouple on an IDOT highway job at 2:00 AM, Lily's 4-person shop cannot provide 24/7 national mobile field service. Graco’s national distributor branches handle this effortlessly.
48:   4. *Severe Margin Dilution:* Sourcing third-party heated hoses, heating elements, and generator trailers compresses gross margins down to <25%, violating Lily's 55%+ corporate margin mandate.
49: * **Approved Alternative Wedge:** Monetize the geotechnical market through single-use injection molded ports (78–86% gross margins) and the unheated "GeoSkid 250" platform for confined-space and cold-foam void filling where Graco trailers cannot fit.
