### 2.1 The Mechanical Sizing & Fluid Ratio Math
Lily's core proportioners (CD-250, CD-200, CD-85, CD-15, and CP-5) rely on dual positive-displacement metering fluid cylinders driven by a common pneumatic power head.

#### 1. Volumetric Ratio Equations
The volumetric ratio ($R$) between Resin Component A and Hardener Component B is governed by cylinder bore area and mechanical stroke length:

$$\text{Displacement Volume}_A = V_A = \frac{\pi \cdot D_A^2}{4} \times L_A$$

$$\text{Displacement Volume}_B = V_B = \frac{\pi \cdot D_B^2}{4} \times L_B$$

$$\text{Mix Ratio } (R) = \frac{V_A}{V_B} = \left(\frac{D_A}{D_B}\right)^2 \times \left(\frac{L_A}{L_B}\right)$$

* *Fixed Stroke Systems (Direct Drive Linkage):* $L_A = L_B \implies R = \left(\frac{D_A}{D_B}\right)^2$
* *Variable Ratio Systems (Rocker Arm / Lever Beam Linkage):* Stroke length ratio $\frac{L_A}{L_B}$ is adjusted mechanically along the fulcrum pin positions:

$$\frac{L_A}{L_B} = \frac{X_{\text{pivot}\to A}}{X_{\text{pivot}\to B}}$$

#### 2. Pneumatic Sizing & Pressure Amplification Formula
To inject structural epoxies into hairline cracks (0.005" to 0.020") or deep bridge joints, fluid pressures must achieve $500 \text{ to } 2,500 \text{ PSI}$ using standard jobsite air compressors delivering $90 \text{ to } 120 \text{ PSI}$ plant air.

The air motor drive cylinder bore ($D_{\text{air}}$) must overcome fluid resistance across both metering cylinders:

$$F_{\text{drive}} = P_{\text{in}} \times \left(\frac{\pi \cdot D_{\text{air}}^2}{4}\right) \times \eta_{\text{mechanical}} \quad (\text{where } \eta_{\text{mech}} \approx 0.88)$$

$$F_{\text{fluid}} = P_{\text{fluid}} \times \left(\frac{\pi \cdot D_A^2}{4} + \frac{\pi \cdot D_B^2}{4}\right)$$

$$\text{Required Air Motor Bore } (D_{\text{air}}) \ge \sqrt{\frac{P_{\text{fluid}} \cdot (D_A^2 + D_B^2)}{P_{\text{in}} \cdot \eta_{\text{mech}}}}$$

#### 3. Viscosity Compensation & Valve Sizing Heuristic
* **Low Viscosity Epoxies ($< 500\text{ cps}$):** Ball check valves with light spring loads ($0.5\text{ to } 1.0\text{ PSI}$ cracking pressure), PTFE packings.
* **Medium Viscosity Formulations ($500 - 5,000\text{ cps}$):** Standard stainless poppet valves, medium springs ($3.0\text{ PSI}$), filled UHMW-PE packings.
* **High Viscosity Gels & Pastes ($> 5,000\text{ cps}$):** Positive spring-return spool valves, enlarged intake manifold bores (minimum $0.75''\text{ ID}$), follower plates on supply tanks, and heavy-duty polyurethane chevron packings.

### 2.3 The Proprietary Injection Molding Consumable Engine

#### 1. Mold Maintenance & Shot Cost Amortization Formula
From historical P&L Account 5440 (*Repairs & Maintenance - Molds*), mold tooling requires periodic maintenance (polishing, ejector pin replacement, runner recutting, cavity refurbishment).

$$\text{Cost Per Part} = \left(\frac{\text{Shot Weight (grams)} \times \text{Resin Price per Gram}}{1 - \text{Scrap Rate}}\right) + \left(\frac{\text{Press Machine Hour Rate}}{\text{Parts per Hour}}\right) + \text{Tooling PM Reserve}$$

Where:
* **Virgin Resin Price:** Nylon 6/6, Acetal (POM), or Polypropylene at bulk pellet pricing ($\$1.85 \text{ to } \$3.20/\text{lb}$).
* **Tooling PM Reserve:** Jack models a mandatory **$\$0.015 \text{ per port}$** reserve accrued directly into an escrow account for tooling refurbishment to prevent surprise $30,000 mold rebuild write-downs (as seen in historical 2024–2025 financials).
* **Consumable Pricing Rules:** Never discount entry ports below $0.48/unit even on large pallet orders (bulk bags of 500). List price targets: $0.75 to $1.15/unit depending on port neck geometry and check valve ball inclusion.
