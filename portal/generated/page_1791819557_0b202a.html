Federal & State Specification Reality Check

How Federal BABA, EPA AIS (American Iron and Steel), and State DOT Qualified Product Lists (QPL) actually treat geotechnical polyurethane injection consumables:

Regulatory Framework Statutory Reach on Packers / Ports Formulator Compliance Status Lily Competitive Moat Impact
Build America, Buy America (BABA)
Title IX, IIJA P.L. 117-58		 Applies to permanent iron, steel, and manufactured products incorporated into permanent works. Temporary injection ports removed or flush-cut post-cure are routinely classified as exempt construction supplies. Avanti and NCFI rely on FHWA general waivers for non-structural consumables. No current audit pressure. ZERO MANDATORY PULL. Formulators will not pay domestic premium when import waivers apply.
EPA American Iron & Steel (AIS)

Distributors carry small domestic batches strictly for certified bid riders; 85%+ volume remains Asian imports. MINOR NICHE WEDGE. High administrative paperwork cost for negligible spot volume.
State DOT QPL / APL Specifications
Individual State Highway Specs		 Chemical resin is spec-approved (e.g., ASTM C881 or specific urethane foam density). The delivery port/packer is almost universally treated as contractor option provided pressure is maintained. Formulators certify their liquid chemical. They explicitly leave mechanical packer brand selection to the blasting/drilling contractor. ZERO LOCKOUT. Formulators lack incentive to lock themselves to Lily when contractors buy cheap generic wands.
Diligence Truth: State DOT specifications almost never lock down injection consumable brands for polyurethane void filling. Trying to force a private-label formulator OEM agreement using regulatory compliance leverage is legally and commercially baseless.
