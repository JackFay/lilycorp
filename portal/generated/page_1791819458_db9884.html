Why Not Injection Mold the Adapter Body? Injection molding a plastic coupler requires a new $18,000–$25,000 steel mold base to withstand 3,000+ PSI hydrostatic injection pressure without blowing off the wand. Using domestic machined CA360 brass reduces upfront capex to <$1,500 while offering indestructible durability that civil general contractors trust on messy highway jobsites.
